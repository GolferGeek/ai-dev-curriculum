# Curriculum readiness assessment

Assessment date: 2026-08-06

## Decision

The offering is marketable now as a five-day, developer-only AI development
program and is ready for a pilot or founding client cohort.

It should not be represented as a zero-preparation, universally configured
course or as a source of production-ready reference applications. The durable
curriculum is complete. Client decisions, live accounts, approved services,
current tool terrain, and instructor rehearsal are intentionally resolved
during cohort preparation.

## What the program now offers

The week forms one coherent progression:

1. build a useful product from intention and acceptance criteria;
2. cross real persistence, authentication, and data-isolation boundaries;
3. install an evidence-driven quality loop;
4. understand unfamiliar code before changing it;
5. reason about observable agent-to-agent delegation;
6. scout, evaluate, govern, publish, and maintain skills and agents;
7. evaluate models against the client's own work; and
8. leave behind an owned AI development operating system.

Learners use one continuing codebase. They build in `apps/`, can compare or
recover from complete implementations in `completed/apps/`, and maintain
canonical organizational capabilities in `ai/`. Generated Claude Code,
Cursor, and Codex interpretations make the approach portable without pretending
that the three harnesses are identical.

## Why the AI program is a material differentiator

`docs/ai-program/` is not a course handout archive. It is an agent-readable
operating memory for the client's continuing development decisions.

An agent can use it to answer:

- what skills and specialized agents the organization approves and how they
  are sourced, reviewed, published, updated, and retired;
- which harnesses and models are allowed for which work, including the
  consequences of provider lock-in and data handling;
- when agent-to-agent delegation is appropriate and what identity, authority,
  protocol, observability, and failure requirements apply;
- what coding governance, evidence, review, testing, and LLM-usage rules apply;
- which security, privacy, delivery, adoption, and measurement decisions are
  approved, proposed, missing, stale, or in conflict; and
- how to modernize the documentation without silently rewriting policy.

The answer contract requires citations, decision status, scope, owner,
evidence date, uncertainty, and a next action. The change contract requires a
proposal or decision record, review, freshness metadata, and synchronized
canonical/projection updates. This lets a client ask useful questions today
and safely evolve the record later.

## Known limits and honest claims

- No document can substitute for the client's owners, risk appetite, accounts,
  or data policy. Unassigned client decisions are visible by design.
- A human instructor rehearsal has not been performed by automation. The
  schedule is well designed and internally consistent, but real timing should
  be captured during rehearsal and the first cohort.
- Authenticated SurrealDB and live-provider exercises depend on approved
  client infrastructure and must be rehearsed in that environment.
- The root and all 15 JavaScript reference packages have zero high-severity npm
  audit findings at the assessment date; package and advisory state remains
  dated terrain and must be refreshed before a cohort.
- OPML structure can be generated and validated; MindNode presentation style
  should be calibrated by the instructor after importing the first map.
- Tool interfaces, model availability, pricing, and vendor strategy are dated
  terrain. Marketing and teaching should describe the decision framework, not
  promise permanent vendor behavior.

## Go-to-market language

Appropriate:

> A five-day, developer-only program that combines hands-on AI application
> delivery with the organizational skills, agents, governance, model
> evaluation, and living decision system needed to keep improving after the
> course.

Avoid:

- guaranteed build times or identical outcomes on every environment;
- claims that reference applications are production-ready;
- promises that one IDE, harness, model, or provider will remain the best;
- statements that client governance is complete before discovery; and
- claims that a generated deck or map replaces instructor preparation.

## Release gate for a scheduled cohort

The program can be sold while these delivery gates are being completed, but
the instructor should not open Day 1 until all are green:

- client audience, topology, accessibility, accounts, network, and source
  access confirmed;
- approved harness, model, data, agent-authority, and code-review policy
  recorded in the AI program;
- dated terrain, protocols, packages, and open advisories refreshed;
- clean clone plus service-backed technical rehearsal completed;
- decks re-rendered after any edit;
- one timed instructor run completed, with Day 3 compression decisions noted;
- MindNode maps imported and instructor-calibrated; and
- recovery references, offline fixtures, and escalation contacts ready.

## Final assessment

This is no longer a collection of promising starter documents. It is a
coherent, supportable course product with an unusually strong post-course
operating system. Market it now for a carefully prepared developer cohort,
collect timing and learner evidence from the pilot, and use the AI program
itself to govern subsequent modernization.
