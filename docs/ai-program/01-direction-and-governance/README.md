# Direction and governance

This category explains why the AI program exists, where it applies, who may
decide, and how approved positions become durable organizational policy. It is
the root for interpreting every other category: technical, risk, compliance,
and operational records have no reliable meaning without scope, authority, and
precedence.

The organization records its current stance here, distinguishing active policy
from proposals, course defaults, local preferences, and unresolved decisions.

## Nodes

- [Purpose, principles, and outcomes](01-purpose-principles-and-outcomes/README.md)
- [Scope, layers, and precedence](02-scope-layers-and-precedence/README.md)
- [Roles, ownership, and decision rights](03-roles-ownership-and-decision-rights/README.md)
- [Policy, decisions, and exceptions](04-policy-decisions-and-exceptions/README.md)
- [Oversight and accountability](05-oversight-and-accountability/README.md)

This category is incomplete when purpose, scope, authority, ownership, or
precedence cannot be determined from approved records.
