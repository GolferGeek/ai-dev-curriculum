# Policy, decisions, and exceptions

This node explains how the organization records current rules, preserves
decision history, distinguishes proposals from active policy, and grants
bounded exceptions without erasing the governing requirement.

The company stance should link normative categories and nodes, accepted and superseded
decisions, proposals, exception records, compensating controls, approvers,
effective and expiration dates, and revocation or rollback paths. The durable
decision log lives under [Program evolution](../../07-program-evolution/02-proposals-decisions-and-supersession/README.md).

The program agent reports silent policy changes, missing supersession,
unapproved or expired exceptions, and descriptive guidance incorrectly treated
as mandatory policy.
