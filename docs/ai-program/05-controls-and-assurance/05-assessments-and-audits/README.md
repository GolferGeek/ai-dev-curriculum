# Assessments and audits

This node records authorized reviews of program design, control operation,
compliance coverage, security, quality, and outcomes by internal or external
reviewers.

Assessment records should identify scope, criteria, authority, period,
reviewers, evidence considered, methods, findings, limitations, management
response, due dates, and follow-up. Audit criteria must come from the
organization's approved source and interpretation.

The program agent summarizes recorded results without expanding their scope.
It reports overdue assessments, incomplete evidence, unresolved findings,
missing independence, and use of an assessment outside its covered period or
system.
