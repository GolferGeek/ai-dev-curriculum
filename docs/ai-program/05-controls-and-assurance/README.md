# Controls and assurance

This category connects approved requirements and risk treatment to preventive,
detective, and corrective controls, their real enforcement surfaces, durable
evidence, testing, assessment, deficiencies, and verified remediation.

A written rule is not automatically a technical control, and the existence of
a control does not prove that it operated effectively.

- [Enforcement surfaces and guardrails](02-enforcement-surfaces-and-guardrails/README.md)
- [Evidence and retention](03-evidence-and-retention/README.md)
- [Control testing and continuous assurance](04-control-testing-and-continuous-assurance/README.md)
- [Assessments and audits](05-assessments-and-audits/README.md)
- [Deficiencies, remediation, and verification](06-deficiencies-remediation-and-verification/README.md)

This category is incomplete when a requirement or material risk has no owned
control, the control's enforcement is unclear, or current evidence cannot show
whether it ran.
