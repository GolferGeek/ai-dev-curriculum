## Context and trigger

What changed or what problem requires a decision?

## Current state

What is active today? Link governing documents and decisions.

## Decision

What will become true if approved? Use explicit must, should, and may language.

## Options considered

Include “keep the current state” and explain tradeoffs.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Link dated primary sources and internal evaluations. Label inference.

## Security, data, cost, and operational impact

Cover permissions, data movement, provider terms, spend, support, and failure.

## Migration and rollback

Name phases, consumers, compatibility, recovery, and the rollback signal.

## Affected documents, capabilities, and systems

List exact paths and owners.

## Validation and outcome measures

Define pass, fail, observation period, and who judges.

## Review triggers

Set a date and events that reopen the decision.
