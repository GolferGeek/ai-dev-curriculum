# AI literacy, training, and coaching

This node defines the knowledge and demonstrated abilities people need for the
AI responsibilities assigned to them. Completion of a course is evidence of
attendance, not proof that someone can recognize limitations, protect data,
verify work, exercise authority correctly, or respond to failure.

The company stance should define role-based learning outcomes, prerequisites,
practice environments, accessibility, assessment, coaching, refresher cadence,
and who may perform higher-risk work. Training must follow current policy and
tools; obsolete instruction is a program defect.

## Required records

- role-to-competency matrix and required learning paths;
- approved examples, exercises, and prohibited-data boundaries;
- observed assessments and remediation criteria;
- coach or champion responsibilities and support capacity;
- completion, demonstrated-competence, and refresher evidence; and
- process for updating training after policy, tool, risk, or incident change.

## Ask this node

- What must this role understand and demonstrate before using AI here?
- Which training is required for this risk tier or authority level?
- Does evidence show competence or only completion?
- Who supports a person when the approved workflow fails?

## Findings and review triggers

Report generic training applied to materially different roles, completion-only
metrics, outdated examples, unsafe practice data, inaccessible delivery,
unavailable coaching, or authority granted before demonstrated competence.
Re-review after policy or tool change, incident, assessment failure, new role,
new risk tier, or recurring support need.
