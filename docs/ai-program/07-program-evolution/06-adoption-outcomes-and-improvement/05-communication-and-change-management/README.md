# Communication and change management

This node governs how the organization explains AI-related decisions, changes,
limits, responsibilities, and consequences to affected people. Communication
does not manufacture consent or convert an unapproved proposal into policy.

The company stance should define audiences, messengers, timing, accessible
formats, consultation, feedback routes, decision transparency, and how changed
expectations enter training, procedures, controls, and support. Messages must
separate current policy, pilot, proposal, external terrain, and uncertainty.

## Required records

- stakeholder and affected-population map;
- change narrative including benefits, costs, risks, and non-goals;
- communication and consultation plan with accessible channels;
- frequently asked questions tied to governing records;
- feedback, response, and decision log; and
- rollout, pause, rollback, and follow-through responsibilities.

## Ask this node

- Who is affected and what do they need to know before the change?
- Which parts are decided, proposed, experimental, or still unknown?
- How may affected people influence or challenge the decision?
- Did communications reach people in an accessible and usable form?

## Findings and review triggers

Report announcement-only change, omitted affected groups, overstated certainty,
inaccessible communication, feedback without response, policy communicated
before approval, or material differences between promises and implementation.
Re-review after scope change, delayed rollout, significant feedback, incident,
misinformation, or failure of an expected benefit or protection.
