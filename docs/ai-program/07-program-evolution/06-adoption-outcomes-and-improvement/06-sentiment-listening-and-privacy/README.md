# Sentiment, listening, and privacy

This node governs approved methods for understanding confidence, concern,
frustration, trust, perceived pressure, support demand, and lived experience
around AI. Sentiment is contextual evidence, not objective truth, compliance
proof, individual performance data, or permission to monitor people silently.

The company stance should identify a legitimate purpose, approved collection
methods, voluntariness or other authorized basis, notice, anonymity or
confidentiality limits, minimum reporting groups, access, retention, deletion,
prohibited uses, analysis boundaries, and how findings lead to visible action.
Free text may contain sensitive, personal, client, or incident information and
requires an approved handling path.

## Required records

- approved questions, methods, population, cadence, and purpose;
- privacy, employment, accessibility, security, and consultation review;
- data-flow map, processor or survey-tool assessment, and retention schedule;
- minimum cohort and suppression rules to reduce re-identification;
- access, analysis, publication, deletion, and incident controls;
- prohibited individual scoring, performance, disciplinary, or covert uses;
- limitations, response bias, missing populations, and confidence statement;
  and
- aggregate findings, decisions, actions, owners, and follow-up dates.

## Interpretation rules

- Combine sentiment with interviews, support demand, observed workflow,
  quality, incident, workload, and outcome evidence.
- Segment only when approved, necessary, and large enough to protect people.
- Do not infer emotion, intent, protected characteristics, or individual risk
  from messages, activity logs, or model-generated labels without an approved
  requirement, validation, and human-governed process.
- Report non-response, sampling, power, timing, and social-desirability limits.
- Preserve critical and minority views; an average must not erase a safety
  signal.

## Ask this node

- Why are we collecting this information, and who approved that purpose?
- Can a person be identified directly or through a small cohort?
- Who may see raw responses, derived labels, and aggregate results?
- Which uses are prohibited, and when will the data be deleted?
- What changed because people provided feedback?

## Findings and review triggers

Report sentiment collection without authority or notice, unverifiable
anonymity claims, small-group disclosure, excessive retention, covert or
individual monitoring, unvalidated automated inference, free text in an
unapproved system, ignored minority concerns, or repeated collection without
visible follow-through. Restrict collection or reporting until the accountable
privacy, people, security, and program owners resolve material gaps.

Re-review before a new collection, tool, question set, population, purpose,
segmentation, model-based analysis, disclosure, retention change, or after a
privacy incident, complaint, works-council or representative concern, or
evidence that respondents do not trust the process.
