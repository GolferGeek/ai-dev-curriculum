# Risk management

This category explains how the organization identifies, classifies, treats,
monitors, accepts, and escalates AI-related risk. Risk is not a synonym for
prohibition: the organization states what it accepts, mitigates, transfers,
avoids, or restricts pending an authorized decision.

owners, required treatment, monitoring, or authorized acceptance.
