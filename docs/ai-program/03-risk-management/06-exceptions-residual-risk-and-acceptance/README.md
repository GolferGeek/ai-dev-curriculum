# Exceptions, residual risk, and acceptance

This node records what risk remains after controls, who may accept it, and how
a bounded exception is approved without becoming an undocumented bypass.

The company stance should link the governing requirement or control, business
reason, scope, risk, compensating controls, evidence, owners, approver, start
and expiration dates, triggers, and revocation or containment.

The program agent reports expired, ownerless, overly broad, or unapproved
exceptions; missing compensating controls; repeated exceptions indicating
policy failure; and acceptance by an unauthorized role.
