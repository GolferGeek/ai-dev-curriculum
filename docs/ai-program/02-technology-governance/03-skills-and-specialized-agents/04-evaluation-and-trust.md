# Evaluation and trust

Evaluate an exact revision:

- Problem and organizational relevance
- Source, maintainer, license, and provenance
- Capability versus preference
- Instruction quality and maturity
- Trigger precision and overlap
- Scripts, tools, network, filesystem, and secrets
- Dependencies and compatibility
- Context cost
- Tests and known failure modes
- Recommended scope and owner

Recommendations: reject, learn-and-rebuild, sandbox, approve with modifications, or approve as-is. Confidence is a claim; attach evidence and uncertainty.
