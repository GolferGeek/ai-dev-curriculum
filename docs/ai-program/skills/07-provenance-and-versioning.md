# Provenance and versioning

Record publisher, source URL, commit/hash, license, discovery date, content hash, reviewed revision, internal modifications, modifier, reason, approval history, installations, and upstream changes.

Approval applies to the reviewed revision—not permanently to a name.

Adoption choices: use as-is, configure, wrap, fork, rebuild, or reject. Preserve upstream lineage and make local divergence visible. Any changed instructions, scripts, permissions, or dependencies can require re-review.
