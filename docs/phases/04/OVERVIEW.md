# Phase 04 — Overview

## Today

You will study the shift from agents that use tools to agents that represent people and companies, then build and inspect a working agent-to-agent system.

## The thesis

> Agent-to-agent communication is the next required business interface.

In the near future, a company will not be fully reachable merely because humans can browse its website or call its API. Customers, candidates, vendors, and partners will increasingly arrive through authorized agents. Every organization will need to decide what its agent can discover, negotiate, approve, buy, sell, disclose, and escalate.

The smallest version is an agent-friendly website that returns clean Markdown or structured data instead of navigation, ads, and presentation chrome. The important version is much larger: persistent agents representing both sides of a business relationship.

## Learn

- Why agent-to-agent systems are a business transition, not merely a new integration technique
- The difference between an agent using a tool and an agent delegating to another autonomous actor
- How A2A, MCP, AP2, x402, and AG-UI address different layers
- How companies expose capabilities without exposing internal reasoning or proprietary systems
- Why identity, authority, reputation, consent, rate limits, and audit trails become core product features
- How agent-native marketplaces can reward fit instead of scattershot volume
- How to verify agent behavior by reading the wire

## Leave with

- A working multi-agent protocol dashboard
- A plain-language map of the protocol landscape
- One inspectable discovery, delegation, authorization, payment, and UI-streaming flow
- A first draft of how a real organization would become agent-reachable
- A trust and anti-abuse checklist for an agent-facing service

## Session arc

| Act | Purpose |
|---|---|
| The next business interface | Establish why this phase is the most consequential one |
| Tools versus counterparties | Separate MCP-style capability use from A2A delegation |
| The protocol layer cake | Locate discovery, collaboration, authorization, settlement, and observability |
| Agent-native markets | Examine jobs, vendors, customers, reputation, and anti-spam design |
| Build and inspect | Run Discover / Ask / Explore and verify the wire |
| Design for your company | Define the first agent-facing capability and its boundaries |
