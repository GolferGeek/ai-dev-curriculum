# Demo-grade bar — Phase 06

## Pass

The demonstration:

- discovers or loads the configured model roster,
- lets the learner choose contestants, judges, prompts, and run settings,
- sends prompts and captures responses,
- records timing and token-speed metrics,
- updates results during the run,
- shows Round 1 scores and consistency,
- supports Round 2 comparison,
- drills into raw responses and judge commentary,
- survives missing models and partial runs,
- persists results and exports the Markdown report.

## Fail

The demonstration fails when:

- model names are presented as timeless truth,
- unavailable models crash the whole run,
- judges score themselves,
- results disappear after restart,
- the dashboard hides raw responses,
- speed or consistency is omitted,
- or the class treats the podium as a universal answer.

Model routing, reasoning-level selection, and harness comparison are discussion topics, not demo requirements.
