# Phase 06 — Overview

## Today

Build the established Model Evaluation Lab: configurable contestants, prompts, repeated runs, multiple judges, live results, drilldown, and an exportable report.

## Leave with

- A working evaluation harness and dashboard
- Workload-specific quality, speed, and consistency evidence
- Experience comparing local and hosted models
- A clearer understanding of AI-judge strengths and failure modes
- A vocabulary for discussing utility, workhorse, specialist, and deep-thinker models
- A high-level understanding of model routing and why the category matters

## Central lesson

> Evaluate models on your work. Use the results to discuss the right level of model for each job—not to crown a universal winner.
