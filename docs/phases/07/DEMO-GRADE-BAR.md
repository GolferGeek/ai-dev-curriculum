# Demo-grade bar — Phase 07

## Pass

The demonstration:

- derives its navigation from `docs/ai-program/` folders;
- renders every selected folder's README and authority metadata;
- shows all eight categories and the exact findings behind readiness;
- switches among Full, Essential, and Light while retaining one canonical
  source and eight-category model;
- explains why a profile is a view, not an exemption, and demonstrates one
  expansion trigger;
- answers an AI-culture question while treating sentiment as privacy-bounded
  aggregate evidence rather than individual performance data;
- distinguishes active, draft, proposed, example, external, and missing
  information;
- answers at least one canonical health question with scope, citations, owner,
  freshness, uncertainty, and next action;
- reports an unanswerable question as a bounded gap;
- displays a requirement-to-control trace through all required links;
- exposes missing approval, evidence, exception, or freshness without claiming
  compliance;
- prepares a reviewable proposal without mutating active policy;
- completes one low-risk change with classification, tests, review evidence,
  rollback/recovery, and result;
- passes application and program checks; and
- names 30/60/90-day owners and review dates.

## Fail

The demonstration fails when:

- the application becomes a second source of policy truth;
- navigation and folder content can drift independently;
- a course default or fictional trace is presented as client policy;
- a score is described as compliance, certification, or legal sufficiency;
- a smaller profile hides an applicable requirement, risk, active decision, or
  stricter overlay;
- sentiment becomes covert monitoring, individual scoring, or an unsupported
  claim about workforce culture;
- citations omit scope, authority, freshness, or uncertainty;
- absence is interpreted as permission;
- a proposal silently edits or activates policy;
- sensitive evidence is copied into Git without authorization;
- technical decisions have no risk/control trace where consequences require
  one;
- only a static hero screen exists; or
- the team presents chat output without running behavior and evidence.

## Stretch

After passing, add an authorized search/index projection, access-controlled
evidence adapters, change comparison, scoped overlays, approval integration,
event-driven freshness checks, control-test ingestion, or a hosted/local model
adapter that preserves the answer contract.
