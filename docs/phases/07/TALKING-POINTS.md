# Phase 07 — Talking points
## The product is the program, not the screen

- The organization's durable asset is its accumulated decisions, rationale,
  ownership, controls, evidence pointers, exceptions, and review history.
- The application makes that asset usable company-wide. It remains a
  rebuildable projection, not a second policy database.
- A folder README is valuable because GitHub, documentation sites, IDEs,
  agents, and the application can all retrieve the same stance.
- Portability is deliberate: the organization may relocate the program or
  separate protected evidence without abandoning the information model.

## Governance, GRC, and technical direction

- AI governance is the umbrella for purpose, authority, accountability, risk,
  compliance, technical choices, controls, operations, and evolution.
- GRC supplies the requirement, risk, control, evidence, assurance, exception,
  and review trace.
- Models, harnesses, skills, agents, tools, MCP, A2A, routing, spend, and
  architecture belong under technology governance.
- A technical decision enters a GRC trace when its data, authority, operational,
  contractual, security, financial, or compliance consequences matter.
- Avoid two competing trees called “AI Governance” and “AI Technical.” One
  program with explicit branches creates clearer precedence and ownership.

## Honest readiness

- A polished screen can make a weak record look authoritative. Surface status,
  scope, owner, approver, freshness, evidence, and uncertainty at the point of
  use.
- “Draft,” `UNASSIGNED`, and “no evidence pointer” are correct initial states.
- A dashboard score is a deterministic record-readiness indicator, not a
  compliance rating or maturity certification.
- No scheduled review is overdue does not mean the content is current. Events
  can invalidate it earlier.
- Missing policy is not permission. Keep consequential action restricted while
  the authorized owner resolves the gap.

## Qualified program answers

Every consequential answer includes:

1. current answer and applicable scope;
2. governing sources and their authority state;
3. owner, approver, last review, and next review;
4. exceptions, conflicts, and stricter overlays;
5. uncertainty and missing links; and
6. the safest next action.

Retrieval and citations are not enough. The advisor must explain why a source
governs and whether the requested action can proceed. Conversely, a fluent
model is not an authority merely because it can summarize external frameworks.

## Controls and evidence

- A policy statement describes intent; an enforcement surface changes what
  people or systems can actually do.
- Instructions, IAM, branch protection, CI, runtime checks, independent review,
  and manual procedures offer different assurance.
- Evidence must be durable, attributable, scoped, retained, and inspectable.
- A test proves the behavior it exercised—not every condition in production.
- One complete high-consequence trace teaches more than a hundred orphaned
  controls or imported framework rows.

## Human authority and change

- An agent may assess records and prepare a proposal within scope.
- It may update active policy only after explicit authorized approval and must
  preserve history.
- Legal interpretation, compliance certification, risk acceptance, exception
  approval, external commitments, and destructive changes remain bounded by
  named human authority.
- Policy and executable capabilities should change together when behavior
  depends on the decision.
- “The agent updated the doc” is not a governance process. The review record,
  authority, validation, supersession, and follow-up make it one.

## Take-home ownership

- The course provides a complete starting graph and working interface.
- The client supplies its actual sources, owners, approved decisions, systems,
  and evidence over time.
- The adoption worksheets are intake tools; completed decisions move into the
  program rather than remaining a separate binder.
- The program succeeds when a developer and an authorized owner reach the same
  qualified answer from the same record.
