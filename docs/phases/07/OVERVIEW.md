# Phase 07 — Learner overview
## Today

You will make the organization's started AI Program visible and useful. The
program is the record the company keeps; the application is how people browse,
assess, trace, and ask questions of that record.

## You will do

1. Baseline the repository and identify what may be changed safely.
2. Inspect the standard eight-category AI Program graph.
3. Assign real owners and scopes where the client has authorized answers.
4. Preserve unknowns, conflicts, and missing evidence as findings.
5. Run the Governance & GRC reference and confirm its navigation comes from
   the folders.
6. Ask the six canonical health questions and inspect the citations.
7. Complete one requirement-to-control trace.
8. Prepare, review, and—only with authority—apply one bounded program proposal.
9. Run one low-risk software or capability change through the governed loop.
10. Present the product, residual gaps, and 30/60/90-day owners.

## You will leave with

- a take-home AI Program that remains readable without the app;
- a working company-wide interface over it;
- a repeatable method for finding stale, missing, or conflicting governance;
- a safe proposal and approval boundary;
- evidence that one change followed the declared controls; and
- a concrete next-review schedule.

## Central lesson

> Governance becomes useful when the same organizational record can guide a
> developer, answer an executive, support a reviewer, and still admit what it
> does not know.
