# Demo-grade bar — Phase 05

Pass when the catalog refreshes from at least three configured sources, preserves provenance and exact revision, diffs runs, searches full content, previews supporting files, filters the skills matrix, and supports three evidence-backed evaluations including one rejection.

Fail when counts are hard-coded as timeless, raw popularity becomes trust, scripts are hidden, revision is unknown, “copy” implies approval, or discovered skills are silently installed.
