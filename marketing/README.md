# Marketing Materials

This folder contains draft proposal, marketing, and syllabus material for packaging the AI Development Curriculum as a business-facing course offering.

## Core Positioning

**AI-Accelerated Software Development**  
*Helping small teams build like big teams.*

The flagship five-day course is strictly for software developers and hands-on
technical leads who need to deliver more software capability without
immediately adding headcount. A separate one- or two-day offering can serve
non-developer staff.

## Files

- [course-proposal.md](course-proposal.md) - business-facing proposal concept for Hennepin Tech or a similar workforce training partner.
- [hennepin-tech-packet.md](hennepin-tech-packet.md) - the outreach packet: cover email draft, what to attach in what order, and the first-call agenda.
- [marketing-copy.md](marketing-copy.md) - reusable copy for a catalog page, flyer, email, or landing page.
- [course-deliverables.md](course-deliverables.md) - client-facing explanation of what participants leave with: working artifacts, repeatable workflow, and team operating-model documents.
- [decks/phase-00-opening-ai-dev.pptx](decks/phase-00-opening-ai-dev.pptx) - Phase 00 opening PowerPoint for the AI development progression and course framing discussion.
- [decks/phase-02-quality-engineering.pptx](decks/phase-02-quality-engineering.pptx) - Phase 02 opening PowerPoint (quality engineering): generation vs. comprehension, scanner→fixer pairs, the closing bracket, and standards that compound. Matches the Phase 00 deck's visual system; regenerate with [../scripts/build-phase-02-deck.py](../scripts/build-phase-02-deck.py).
- [decks/phase-03-project-memory.pptx](decks/phase-03-project-memory.pptx) - Phase 03 opening PowerPoint: brownfield investigation, durable project memory, and promotion of verified findings.
- [decks/phase-04-agent-to-agent-future.pptx](decks/phase-04-agent-to-agent-future.pptx) - Phase 04 explanatory PowerPoint: a 37-slide teaching story from the first agent visitor through MCP versus A2A, Patron AI, vendor relationships, trust, payments, and the inspectable protocol demo.
- [flyer.html](flyer.html) - a ready-to-share, print-friendly HTML flyer built from the marketing copy (open in a browser; print to PDF for distribution).
- [week-long-syllabus.md](week-long-syllabus.md) - five-day syllabus (**two phases per day** on Days 1–3; Phase 06 + routing on Day 4; adoption / their-repo on Day 5).
- [pre-course-setup.md](pre-course-setup.md) - learner-facing checklist to complete before Day 1 (accounts, installs, platform notes).
- [lesson-plans/](lesson-plans/) - full per-phase teaching lesson plans (intro lecture, intention walk-through, closing discussion). One per curriculum phase (00–06) plus [week-close.md](lesson-plans/week-close.md) for Day 5. Includes [discussion-topics.md](lesson-plans/discussion-topics.md) — cross-cutting instructor talks (model routing, corporate context, SMB operating model) slotted by phase.
- [adoption-kit/](adoption-kit/README.md) - the take-home deliverable: ten fill-in templates that become each team's AI development operating model, including tools, instructions, artifacts, quality, authority, model usage, safety, memory, and organizational ownership.

availability, pricing, data terms, model access, editor/terminal integrations,
and browser/computer-use features. Present vendor strategy and model-provider
