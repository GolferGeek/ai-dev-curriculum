AI-Accelerated Software Development

Helping small teams build like big teams.

A month of meetings and hand-offs becomes a morning — for well-scoped work, when your team knows how to steer, verify, and stay in charge.

Why this course

AI coding agents can now build a well-scoped slice of a business application in under an hour — not every project, only work scoped small enough to finish and verify in one sitting. That changes what your backlog can become — but only for teams that know how to define the work clearly, direct the agents, verify the result, and keep humans responsible for what ships.

This is not a "learn to code with AI" class. It's for smaller teams with big ideas, where the code matters but the business capability matters more: dashboards, customer portals, internal tools, integrations, product prototypes, and the workflow to deliver them safely — again and again.

You will learn how to

  • Turn business ideas into clear intentions, requirements, and plans
  • Direct AI agents to build and modify working applications
  • Build real apps with authentication and per-user data
  • Verify software in a real browser — never trust the transcript
  • Review AI-generated code before it reaches production
  • Research an existing codebase before changing it
  • Package company knowledge as reusable agent skills
  • Pick models with evidence — evals, routing, and cost policy

Who it's for

  • Small business development teams
  • IT and product teams responsible for business applications
  • Technical leads and engineering managers
  • Business analysts working closely with software teams
  • Operations teams building dashboards and automations
  • Developers who want a structured agentic workflow

Every team goes home with a started adoption kit: fill-in templates for tool and plan decisions, shared agent instructions, quality gates, human decision boundaries, model routing and spend policy, and safety rules for your real codebase. It's your organization's AI development operating model — begun in class, finished at work.
