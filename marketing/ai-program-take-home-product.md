# AI Program — take-home product

The course's durable take-home product is a started, company-owned **AI
Program**, together with the skills and application experience needed to use and
maintain it. It is more than the learner codebase and more than a set of
worksheets.

## What the organization receives

### 1. Standard AI Program structure

A folder-backed, agent-readable hierarchy covering:

1. Direction and governance.
2. Technology governance.
3. Risk management.
4. Compliance and obligations.
5. Controls and assurance.
6. Delivery and operations.
7. Program evolution.
8. Program intelligence.

Every category and subcategory folder contains a `README.md`, so the same
structure works in GitHub, documentation sites, IDEs, AI harnesses, and the AI
Governance & GRC application. The canonical hierarchy and node contract are in
[`docs/ai-program/PROGRAM-MAP.md`](../docs/ai-program/PROGRAM-MAP.md).

The same canonical hierarchy supports three machine-readable starting views:

- **Full** for mid-size, large, regulated, multi-client, or higher-authority
  programs;
- **Essential** for a smaller cross-functional organization; and
- **Light** for a small team or bounded low-risk pilot.

Profiles filter navigation, findings, and advisor retrieval; they do not create
separate policy copies or exempt applicable requirements. See the
[profile contract](../docs/ai-program/PROGRAM-PROFILES.md).

### 2. Organization-specific starting content

During the course, participants begin replacing curriculum defaults with their
approved names, scopes, tools, models, data classes, requirements, risk tiers,
controls, evidence locations, owners, exceptions, review dates, and unresolved
gaps. The eleven adoption worksheets are guided intake tools for this work; they
are not a second governance system.

### 3. AI Governance & GRC application

The application provides a company-wide way to browse the folder hierarchy,
view governance health and findings, follow requirement-to-control traces, and
ask the AI Program agent:

- How is our governance?
- What are we lacking or missing?
- What is changing?
- What is old or expiring?
- What conflicts or appears wrong?
- How is our AI culture, and what does approved sentiment evidence actually
  show?
- Which program profile is active, and what would require a broader one?
- What should we address next?

The app reads the approved AI Program and linked evidence metadata. Search
indexes, graph edges, embeddings, and health views are rebuildable projections,
not a competing source of policy.

Program Evolution includes cultural principles, workforce and role impacts,
AI literacy, psychological safety, change communication, privacy-bounded
sentiment listening, and balanced adoption outcomes. Sentiment is aggregate,
qualified evidence—not covert monitoring or individual performance scoring.

### 4. Maintained AI capabilities

Canonical skills and specialized agents help the organization query, assess,
trace, scout, evaluate, publish, maintain, and modernize its AI direction. The
canonical `ai/` source generates supported Cursor, Claude Code, and Codex
projections.

### 5. Reviewable operating model

Git preserves decisions, proposals, ownership, review, and supersession.
Application- or agent-initiated changes remain proposals until the authorized
company process accepts and merges them. Protected contracts, legal advice,
incidents, and audit evidence may remain in approved systems with sanitized
metadata and durable links in the AI Program.

## What is completed on Day 5

The Day 5 goal is a useful starting system, not a claim that organizational
governance or compliance is complete. Teams should leave with:

- the canonical folder structure installed in their chosen repository;
- category pages that explain the program and expose missing company stance;
- named initial scope, program owner, and follow-up owners where available;
- started harness/model, authority, data, risk, control, evidence, exception,
  and review records;
- at least one requirement-to-control trace or a clearly owned gap;
- the application able to browse the hierarchy and answer from approved
  content with citations and uncertainty;
- at least one tested maintenance or advisor capability; and
- a reviewed list of restricted unknowns and Monday follow-through actions.

## Boundaries

The course does not provide legal advice, certify compliance, assign client
risk acceptance, or turn fictional examples into policy. Unassigned ownership,
unknown applicability, missing evidence, and incomplete controls stay visible.
The organization grows the program through authorized effort after the course.

## Portability

The AI Program may live inside an engineering monorepo or in a dedicated
company-wide repository. An organization can move it later without changing
the conceptual model: move the canonical program documents and capabilities,
update consumer repository instructions, regenerate harness projections, and
validate links and query behavior.
