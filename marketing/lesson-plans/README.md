# Lesson Plans

Full teaching lesson plans for the five-day course — one per curriculum phase. Each plan is the **content the instructor delivers**, and doubles as collateral that shows a training partner exactly what a phase teaches.

Every plan follows the same arc:

1. **Intro lecture ("State of the AI Union" for the phase)** — the technology used, the points we're making, why they matter, how that area is changing, and what interesting people are saying about its direction.
2. **Intention walk-through** — what the app is, and how the (already-tuned, not-modified) intention sets the agents up to succeed.
3. **The build** — it runs in well under an hour (Phase 06's *eval run* is the long pole); the instructor keeps teaching while it generates.
4. **Closing discussion** — what we learned this session, and what the participants must leave believing.

These pair with the per-phase `TEACHING.md` (room mechanics) and `README.md`/`RUN-ORDER.md` (exact steps) in each `docs/phase-XX/` folder.

Cross-cutting **business / harness / org** talks (model routing, corporate context, SMB operating model, shadow AI, etc.) live in [discussion-topics.md](discussion-topics.md) — slotted by phase for build-window teaching. The take-home [adoption kit](../adoption-kit/README.md) templates map onto those talks and get filled in during the [week close](week-close.md).

> **Instructor refresh:** anywhere a plan says *[refresh]*, pull a current article, post, or release note before the cohort — the themes are stable, the examples should be current.
