# Skill maintain

Read `docs/ai-program/skills/09-ownership-and-lifecycle.md` and `10-outcomes-and-review.md`.

1. Inventory approved revisions, owners, consumers, destinations, dependencies, review dates, and outcomes.
2. Compare upstream, reviewed, approved, and installed hashes.
3. Flag changed instructions/scripts/permissions, stale approvals, missing owners, failing tests, conflicts, duplicates, and unsupported tools.
4. Review task success, rework, trigger precision, violations, context cost, and overrides.
5. Recommend keep, re-test, re-review, wrap, fork, supersede, restrict, revoke, or retire.
6. For retirement, identify dependents, replacement, communication, migration, and removal verification.
7. Do not update or remove installations without the required approval.

Output: `docs/artifacts/skill-maintenance-report.md`.
